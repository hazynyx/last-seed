# 🌱 LAST SEED

A tiny 2D browser game about saving the Earth. Humanity got a little too obsessed with cyberpunk tech, and the machines took over nature. Your job? Bring the green back.

## 🎮 How to Play
- **Move:** `W`, `A`, `S`, `D`
- **Attack:** `Spacebar` (unlocks after you grow a few trees)

Just double-click `index.html` to open it in any modern browser. No installs, no build steps, no servers.

## 📖 What to Expect
- **Level 1:** Collect yellow seeds and plant them in the dark blocks. Grab water from the ponds to grow them into trees. Once 5 trees are grown, you'll receive a weapon. Defend your forest from 10 attacking robots, then step into the portal to advance.
- **Level 2:** Clean up the area by picking up scattered trash and dropping it at the recycler. Fight off more clankers, then face the final boss to win!

## 📁 Setup & Assets
The game is built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It's set up to load custom sprites from an `assets/` folder and background music from a `musics/` folder. **Missing files?** No worries. The game gracefully falls back to drawing simple shapes and solid colors so it still runs perfectly.
